📖 Flexibility Rule Manual

1. 🔀 Operator Swap

Operators are swapped: + becomes Multiply (×), - becomes Add (+), × becomes Subtract (-).

Example: 5 + 4 under this rule is 5 × 4 = 20.

2. 🔄 Digit Flip

Reverse the digits of all 2-digit numbers before calculating.

Example: 14 + 23 becomes 41 + 32 = 73.

3. ✌️ Even Booster (+2)

Add +2 to any even number in the expression before calculating.

Example: 4 + 5 -> (4+2) + 5 = 6 + 5 = 11.

4. 🎯 Modulo 10 (Last Digit Target)

Calculate normally, but the answer is ONLY the last single digit (Remainder modulo 10).

Example: 14 × 3 = 42 -> Last digit is 2.

5. ➕ Sign Switcher

Invert the sign of numbers (Positive becomes Negative, Negative becomes Positive).

Example: 8 - (-3) becomes (-8) - (+3) = -11.
